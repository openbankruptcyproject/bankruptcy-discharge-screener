Bankruptcy Interactive Tools

Four free client-side calculators. All math runs in your browser — no data collected or sent.

1328(f) Re-Filing Screener

The flagship tool: check whether you can refile bankruptcy under the §1328(f) and §727(a)(8) waiting rules. Self-contained, no login, no email required.

Run screener →

Exemption Calculator

Enter your state and property values. See which bankruptcy exemptions protect your home, vehicle, household goods, and retirement accounts. 50-state coverage.

Check exemptions →

Chapter 7 Means Test

Compare your household income to the 2024 U.S. Trustee median for your state. Fast first-pass: if you pass, Chapter 7 is presumed appropriate.

Run means test →

Chapter 13 Plan Payment Estimator

Enter priority debts, secured arrears, unsecured debt, and disposable income. See your estimated monthly plan payment and unsecured creditor percentage.

Estimate plan →

Credit Rebuild Timeline

Interactive 24-month month-by-month plan for rebuilding credit after discharge. Typical filer recovers 150-200 FICO points by month 24.

Build timeline →

All tools are free, open-source, and client-side. Part of the Open Bankruptcy Project, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (determination pending).
