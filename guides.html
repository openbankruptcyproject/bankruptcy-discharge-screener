Frequently Asked Questions

Are these bankruptcy guides free?

Yes. Every guide in the Bankruptcy Transparency Network is 100% free. There are no paywalls, no registration requirements, and no hidden costs. All content is provided as a public service by the Open Bankruptcy Project, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Who runs the Open Bankruptcy Project?

The Open Bankruptcy Project is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to bankruptcy court transparency and public education. It operates over 100 educational websites covering every major bankruptcy topic, all funded by volunteers and public donations.

Do you collect personal data?

No. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. Our sites use basic analytics to measure traffic but do not track individual users, use cookies for advertising, or share data with third parties.

Is this legal advice?

No. These guides provide general educational information about bankruptcy law. They are not legal advice and do not create an attorney-client relationship. You should consult a qualified bankruptcy attorney for advice about your specific situation.
