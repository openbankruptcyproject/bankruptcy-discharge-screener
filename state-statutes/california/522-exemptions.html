§522 Bankruptcy Exemptions in California

How 11 U.S.C. § 522 applies in California — federal bankruptcy law, California district data.

What §522 Bankruptcy Exemptions Does

Exemptions protect specific property from liquidation. Some states allow debtors to choose between federal and state exemption schedules; others require state schedules. The exemptions available to you depend on your state of residence during the 730 days (2 years) before filing.

Key points:
  • Homestead, motor vehicle, household goods, retirement accounts are typical categories
  • Federal exemption set (§522(d)) available only in opt-out states
  • State-specific dollar amounts vary dramatically (homestead: $5,000 in some states, unlimited in others)
  • §522(f) — lien avoidance on exempt property for judicial liens and some non-PMSIs
