§1325 Chapter 13 Plan Confirmation in Texas

How 11 U.S.C. § 1325 applies in Texas — federal bankruptcy law, Texas district data.

What §1325 Chapter 13 Plan Confirmation Does

Before a Chapter 13 plan is "confirmed" (approved by the court), it must pass six tests: good faith, feasibility, best-interests (unsecureds receive at least what they would in Chapter 7), full priority payment, disposable income commitment, and creditor treatment rules.

Key points:
  • Good faith — both in filing and in plan proposal
  • Feasibility — debtor must be able to make plan payments
  • Best-interests test — unsecureds get no less than Chapter 7 liquidation value
  • §1325(b) disposable-income test with state-median threshold
