§506 Secured Claim Valuation in Nebraska

How 11 U.S.C. § 506 applies in Nebraska — federal bankruptcy law, Nebraska district data.

What §506 Secured Claim Valuation Does

A creditor's claim is "secured" only up to the value of the collateral. Any deficit above that value is unsecured. This framework is the foundation of Chapter 13 cramdown, lien stripping, and lien avoidance.

Key points:
  • Undersecured claim = split into secured (collateral value) + unsecured (deficiency)
  • Enables Chapter 13 cramdown on non-residential real estate and certain vehicle loans
  • §506(d) — unsecured portion of lien can be "stripped off" in Chapter 13
  • Valuation standard varies: replacement value (vehicles under §506(a)(2)) vs. fair market value
