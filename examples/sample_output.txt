================================================================================
  METHODOLOGICAL NOTES / CAVEATS
================================================================================

  1. Name matching is fuzzy (first+last). Some hits may be different people
     with the same name. Manual verification required for each hit.
  2. Pre-BAPCPA cases (Ch.13 filed before 10/17/2005) are EXCLUDED.
     Section 1328(f) did not exist before BAPCPA.
  3. Some 'DISCHARGED' Ch.13 cases may reflect legitimate situations:
     - Court may have determined dates did not overlap
     - Hardship discharge under 1328(b) may not be affected
     - Data entry lag in PACER
  4. 'OPEN/PENDING' status means the case has not yet reached discharge.
     The bar prevents discharge, not filing.
  5. The prior discharge NEED NOT be by a target attorney. The violation
     is filing a Ch.13 that cannot result in discharge.
  6. Attorney competence issue: an attorney should check 1328(f) before
     filing. Filing a case that is discharge-barred wastes the debtor's
     filing fee, attorney fees, and time, while providing no benefit.
  7. Joint cases are split on ' and ' -- each spouse matched independently.
  8. Encoding: utf-8-sig used for CSV reading (handles BOM from PACER).
