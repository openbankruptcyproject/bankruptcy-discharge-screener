What These Numbers Mean

The N.D. Tex. has a dismissal rate of 67.1%, which is significantly above the national average. This means roughly 65,066 of 112,225 Chapter 13 cases filed between 2008 and 2024 were dismissed before debtors could receive a discharge. A high dismissal rate can indicate systemic barriers to plan completion in this district.

Section 1328(f) of the Bankruptcy Code bars Chapter 13 discharge for debtors who received a prior discharge within specified time periods. Prior filers who were dismissed—rather than discharged—are not subject to this bar, but may face other restrictions under Section 109(g).
