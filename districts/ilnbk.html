Bankruptcy in the Northern District of Illinois

Quick answer. Between 1971 and 2026, the searchable federal record shows 486,762 bankruptcy cases filed in the Northern District of Illinois (the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Illinois). Where the filing chapter is recorded, the most common is Chapter 7. This page summarizes what the public data shows about filing here -- the court, the chapters, the judges, and how the district compares to national outcomes. The searchable record is a partial index of historical filings -- coverage is much deeper for recent decades than early ones -- so the count above reflects what is indexed, not the court's lifetime filing volume.

The court

The Northern District of Illinois is served by the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Illinois. The court hears cases in its Eastern Division (Chicago) and Western Division (Rockford).

Of 238,789 Chapter 13 cases filed here between 2008 and 2024, 67,571 ended in a discharge. Section 1328(f) bars a Chapter 13 discharge for debtors who received a prior discharge within specified look-back periods; a prior case that was dismissed rather than discharged does not trigger the 1328(f) bar, though other limits (like Section 109(g)) can apply. How this district compares

Nationally, the FJC Integrated Database of about 8 million bankruptcy terminations analyzed for 2008-2024 shows an overall discharge rate of 58.3% and a dismissal rate of 41.7% (of the 8M analyzed terminations). Outcomes vary sharply by representation: pro-se Chapter 13 debtors reach discharge about 2.9% of the time versus roughly 47% for represented Chapter 13 debtors (FJC 2008-2024).

Frequently asked questions

Which court handles bankruptcy in the Northern District of Illinois? The United States Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Illinois handles federal bankruptcy filings for the district. The court hears cases in its Eastern Division (Chicago) and Western Division (Rockford).

What chapters do people file here? Across the searchable record, filings appear under Chapters 7, 11, 12, and 13. Where a chapter is recorded, Chapter 7 is the most common in the Northern District of Illinois. Chapter 7 (liquidation) and Chapter 13 (individual repayment plan) are the most common nationwide; Chapter 11 (reorganization) is used mainly by businesses and higher-debt individuals; Chapter 12 is for family farmers and fishermen.

How does the Northern District of Illinois compare to the rest of the country? National outcome rates come from the FJC dataset described above (8M terminations, 2008-2024). This page reports the district's own case counts from the searchable federal record; where the district's FJC outcome rate is available it is shown in "How this district compares."

Data and methodology

  • Case counts are of the searchable federal record (a national index of about 9.8 million

cases), filtered to the Northern District of Illinois (source district code ilnbk). This is a count of records in that index, not an official court statistic.

  • Chapter is recorded for 2.1% of these cases; per-chapter counts are floors.
  • Judges and Chapter 11 status come from an enriched dataset of Chapter 11 cases (judge

recorded on a share of them that is data pending for this district); attorney data is sparse for older cases.

  • National rates (58.3% / 41.7%, and the pro-se gap) are from the **FJC Integrated Database,

~8 million terminations analyzed 2008-2024** -- the baseline for all rates on this page. The 9.8M figure is the size of the searchable index only and is never used to compute a rate.

  • Names: individual debtor names are reduced to initials; business names are kept. This page

reports aggregates only.
