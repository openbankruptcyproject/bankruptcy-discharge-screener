Bankruptcy in the Eastern District of Missouri

Quick answer. Between 1982 and 2026, the searchable federal record shows 117,799 bankruptcy cases filed in the Eastern District of Missouri (the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Missouri). Where the filing chapter is recorded, the most common is Chapter 13. This page summarizes what the public data shows about filing here - the court, the filings, and how the district compares to national outcomes. The searchable record is a partial index of historical filings -- coverage is much deeper for recent decades than early ones -- so the count above reflects what is indexed, not the court's lifetime filing volume.

The court

The eastern district of missouri is served by the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Missouri, part of the federal judiciary. Bankruptcy cases under Chapters 7, 11, 12, and 13 are filed and administered there.

How to read these counts. Names are matched as they appear in court records. Surname collisions (two judges sharing a surname) and punctuation or middle-initial variants ("Jane A. Doe" vs "Jane A Doe") can split or merge a single person's cases. Counts cover only cases with a recorded judge. Treat them as indicative, not a docket census.

Of 58,254 Chapter 13 cases filed here between 2008 and 2024, 18,602 ended in a discharge. Section 1328(f) bars a Chapter 13 discharge for debtors who received a prior discharge within specified look-back periods; a prior case that was dismissed rather than discharged does not trigger the 1328(f) bar, though other limits (like Section 109(g)) can apply. Check a specific case with the eligibility checker, or read the plain-language 1328(f) explainer.

How this district compares

Nationally, the FJC Integrated Database of about 8 million bankruptcy terminations analyzed for 2008-2024 shows an overall discharge rate of 58.3% and a dismissal rate of 41.7% (of the 8M analyzed terminations). Outcomes vary sharply by representation: pro-se Chapter 13 debtors reach discharge about 2.9% of the time versus roughly 47% for represented Chapter 13 debtors (FJC 2008-2024).

Frequently asked questions

Which court handles bankruptcy in the Eastern District of Missouri?

The United States Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Missouri handles federal bankruptcy filings for the district.

What do these numbers cover?

Counts on this page come from a public searchable index of federal bankruptcy filings (about 9.8 million cases nationally) plus the FJC Integrated Database for national outcome rates. The index is partial - recent decades are covered far more deeply than early ones - and every percentage on this page states its own denominator.

Where can I check discharge eligibility?

If a prior case ended in discharge, Section 1328(f) look-back periods may bar a new Chapter 13 discharge. The eligibility checker walks through it.
