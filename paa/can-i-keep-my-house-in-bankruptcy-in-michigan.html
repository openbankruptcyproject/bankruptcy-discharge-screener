Can I Keep My House in Bankruptcy in Michigan?

In most cases, yes. The Michigan homestead exemption protects equity in your primary residence up to a statutory limit. Chapter 13 is especially protective of homeownership because it lets you cure mortgage arrears over 60 months while making current payments.
