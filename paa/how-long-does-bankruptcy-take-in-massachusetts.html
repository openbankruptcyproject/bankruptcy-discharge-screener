How Long Does Bankruptcy Take in Massachusetts?

Chapter 7 in Massachusetts typically takes 3-4 months from filing to discharge. Chapter 13 requires 3-5 years of plan payments before discharge. The 341 meeting of creditors is held 21-40 days after filing. Discharge is entered approximately 60 days after the 341 meeting for Chapter 7 cases.
