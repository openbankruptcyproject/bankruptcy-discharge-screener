How to Rebuild Credit After Bankruptcy in Massachusetts?

Three moves: (1) pull your three credit reports, verify all discharged debts show $0 balance, dispute inaccuracies; (2) open a secured credit card and pay in full monthly; (3) add positive rent/utility reporting via services like Experian Boost. Most Massachusetts filers reach 650+ within 24 months.
