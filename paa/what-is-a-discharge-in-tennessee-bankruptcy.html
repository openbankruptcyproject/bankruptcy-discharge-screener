What Is a Discharge in Tennessee Bankruptcy?

A bankruptcy discharge is the court order that eliminates your personal liability for qualifying debts. In Chapter 7 (Tennessee), discharge typically enters 60 days after the 341 meeting. In Chapter 13, after plan completion. Certain debts survive: student loans, most taxes, domestic support, and debts obtained by fraud.
