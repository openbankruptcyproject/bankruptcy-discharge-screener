What Is the Automatic Stay in Arkansas?

The automatic stay (11 U.S.C. § 362) is a federal injunction that applies identically in every state including Arkansas. It takes effect the moment you file bankruptcy, stopping foreclosures, repossessions, wage garnishments, lawsuits, and most collection activity.
