Can I File Bankruptcy Twice in Maryland?

Yes, but federal waiting periods apply. 8 years between Chapter 7 discharges (§727(a)(8)). 4 years from Chapter 7 discharge before Chapter 13 discharge is available (§1328(f)(1)). 2 years between Chapter 13 discharges (§1328(f)(2)). Use the 1328f.com screener to check your eligibility.
