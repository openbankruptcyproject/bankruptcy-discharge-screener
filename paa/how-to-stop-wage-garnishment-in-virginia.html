How to Stop Wage Garnishment in Virginia?

Bankruptcy stops Virginia wage garnishment immediately upon filing via the automatic stay. Other options: file a claim-of-exemption under Virginia law (head-of-household or low-income protection), negotiate a release with the creditor, or challenge garnishment exceeding federal limits (25% of disposable earnings).
