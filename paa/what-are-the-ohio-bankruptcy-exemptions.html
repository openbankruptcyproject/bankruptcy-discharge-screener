What Are the Ohio Bankruptcy Exemptions?

Ohio bankruptcy exemptions protect specific categories of property from liquidation. Core categories: homestead, motor vehicle, household goods, retirement accounts, and wages. See state-specific dollar amounts at bankruptcyexemptionsbystate.com/ohio.
